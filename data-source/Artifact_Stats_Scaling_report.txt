Per Level Increase
Whenever an artifact gains a level the following calculation is applied to its main affix:

AffixValue_Current = AffixValue_Previous + (AffixValue_MainBase × AffixMultiplier)

The AffixMultiplier is unique to each specific quality and Main Affix in question, and varies between 0.28 and 0.29. Also, each attribute is rounded in the UI view.
